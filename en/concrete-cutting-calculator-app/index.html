A calculator app built for concrete cutting and core drilling

Concrete Cutter puts practical job-site calculations for concrete weight, angled drilling, saw overcut, lifting points, slab division and stitch drilling in one mobile toolbox.

Quick answer: Concrete Cutter is a mobile field calculator for concrete cutters and core drillers. Instead of focusing only on concrete volume or material ordering, it combines calculations used around cutting, drilling, lifting and dividing concrete sections on the job site.

More than a generic concrete volume calculator

Many concrete calculators answer one question: how much concrete is needed for a slab, footing or pour. A cutting crew faces a different set of decisions after the concrete is already there.

A cut section may need a weight estimate before lifting. An angled core hole has a longer drilling path and an offset between entry and exit. A circular saw blade may need to travel beyond a corner to complete the cut through the full thickness. A slab may need to be divided into smaller pieces, or an opening may require stitch drilling.

Concrete Cutter brings those field calculations together so an operator can move from measurements to a practical result without opening a collection of unrelated calculators.

Why these calculations belong together

The result of one calculation often becomes part of the next field decision. If a cut piece is too heavy, the next question may be how to divide it. If a hole must be drilled at an angle, drilling path and exit position matter before the rig is set. If a wall-saw cut must finish through a corner, expected overcut matters before marking.

Designed for use beside the saw or drill rig

The app uses short calculation flows, clear results and visual guidance. Core calculations run locally, so the essential tools can be used without depending on job-site connectivity. Metric and imperial measurements are supported, and the interface is available in eight languages.

Who is Concrete Cutter for?

Concrete Cutter is aimed at operators and crews working with concrete sawing, core drilling and related removal planning. It is useful when measurements need to turn into a field decision quickly before a cut, bore, lift or division.

The app does not replace engineering, lifting plans or professional judgement. Reinforcement, embedded services, structural conditions, lifting equipment, rigging, machine limits and local safety requirements must still be checked for the actual job.
