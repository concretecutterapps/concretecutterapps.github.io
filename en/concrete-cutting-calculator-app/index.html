Concrete cutting field calculator

A calculator app built for concrete cutting and core drilling

Concrete Cutter puts practical job-site calculations for concrete weight, angled drilling, saw overcut, lifting points, slab division and stitch drilling in one mobile toolbox.

Quick answer: Concrete Cutter is a mobile field calculator for concrete cutters and core drillers. Instead of focusing only on concrete volume or material ordering, it combines calculations used around cutting, drilling, lifting and dividing concrete sections on the job site.

More than a generic concrete volume calculator

Many concrete calculators answer one question: how much concrete is needed for a slab, footing or pour. A cutting crew faces a different set of decisions after the concrete is already there.

A cut section may need a weight estimate before lifting. A core hole drilled at an angle has a longer drilling path and an offset between entry and exit. A circular wall-saw blade may need to pass beyond a corner to complete the cut through the full thickness. A slab may need to be divided into smaller pieces before removal. An opening that cannot be conventionally sawn may need a stitch-drilling pattern.

Concrete Cutter brings those field calculations together so an operator can move from measurements to a practical result without opening a collection of unrelated calculators.

Why these calculations belong together

The result of one calculation often becomes part of the next field decision. If a cut piece is too heavy, the next question may be how to divide it. If a hole must be drilled through a structure at an angle, the drilling path and exit position matter before the rig is set. If a wall-saw cut must finish through a corner, expected overcut matters before marking and protecting adjacent surfaces.

Concrete Cutter also includes lifting-point calculations, so weight, geometry and removal planning can be handled in the same field toolbox.

Designed for use beside the saw or drill rig

The app uses short calculation flows, clear results and visual guidance rather than office-style estimating screens. Core calculations run locally, so the essential calculation workflow can be used without relying on a job-site internet connection. Store, account or advertising functions can still require a connection.

Metric and imperial measurements are supported. The interface is available in Norwegian, English, Swedish, Danish, German, Polish, Dutch and Finnish.

Who is Concrete Cutter for?

Concrete Cutter is aimed at operators and crews working with concrete sawing, core drilling and related removal planning. It is useful when measurements need to turn into a field decision quickly: before a cut, before a bore, before a section is lifted, or before an opening is divided into workable pieces.

It is not CRM, dispatch or invoicing software, and it does not replace engineering, lifting plans or professional judgement. Reinforcement, embedded services, structural conditions, lifting equipment, rigging, access, machine limits and local safety requirements must still be checked for the actual job.

Frequently asked questions

Is Concrete Cutter a concrete volume calculator?

It can calculate volume and weight as part of its weight tools, but its main focus is concrete cutting and core drilling work rather than estimating concrete for a new pour.

Does Concrete Cutter work offline?

The core calculation tools are designed to run locally without an internet connection. Functions connected to app stores, accounts or advertising may still require a connection.

Does it support metric and imperial measurements?

Yes. The same calculation workflow can be used with metric or imperial units.

Is it available on Android and iPhone?

Yes. Concrete Cutter is available on Google Play and Apple's App Store.

Can it replace a lifting plan or engineering assessment?

No. The app provides practical planning calculations. The real structure, lifting setup, reinforcement, services, access and safety requirements must be assessed for the job.
