Concrete weight calculator

Calculate the approximate weight of a rectangular concrete piece from length, width, thickness and density directly in your browser.

How concrete weight is calculated

The calculator finds the volume from the entered dimensions and multiplies it by the material density. Around 2,400 kg/m³ is a common planning value for normal concrete, but actual density varies with the mix, reinforcement and material. Use project-specific data whenever it is available.

Useful before cutting and lifting

A quick weight estimate helps when planning how a cut section will be lifted, transported or divided into smaller pieces. Weight is only one part of the job: lifting points, centre of gravity, equipment capacity, access and local safety requirements still need to be assessed for the actual site.

Need more shapes?

Concrete Cutter includes additional shapes, material choices, calculation history and field tools for concrete cutting and core drilling. The web calculator stays deliberately simple for fast use from any browser.
