Frequently asked questions

Can the app be used offline?

Yes. Core calculations run locally. Internet may be needed for ads, purchases, analytics, crash reporting and Premium verification.

Which units are supported?

The app supports metric and imperial units. Your selected unit is stored locally.

Which languages are supported?

Norwegian, English, Swedish, Danish, German, Polish, Dutch and Finnish.

Where is history stored?

History, notes and settings are stored locally on the device and are not sent to a Concrete Cutter account or server.

Is history lost when uninstalling?

It may be lost if app data is deleted or the app is uninstalled. History is not automatically synced between devices.

Are results a guarantee or formal documentation?

No. The app is a practical calculation tool. Users must verify dimensions, materials, assumptions, loads and safety requirements before use.

What does Premium include?

Premium unlocks features marked as Premium and removes ads according to the offer shown in the app.

How do I restore Premium?

Use the same Google Play account or Apple Account used for the purchase, ensure internet access and restart the app. The platform must be able to verify the purchase.

Why are no ads shown?

Ads may be temporarily unavailable due to network conditions, ad inventory or processing by the ad service. Core calculations are unaffected.

How do I report a possible calculation error?

Send the feature, values, units, material, expected result, shown result, app version and phone model to concretecutter.app@gmail.com.

Is the app available for iPhone?

Yes. Concrete Cutter is available for both Android and iPhone.
