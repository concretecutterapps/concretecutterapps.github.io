Concrete slab division

Plan how a concrete slab or cut section can be divided into smaller, more manageable pieces using dimensions and estimated weight.

Start with total weight

Before dividing a slab, estimate its total weight and geometry. Thickness, area and material density determine the theoretical mass, while reinforcement and local material variations can change the real value.

Division is about more than kilograms

A target piece weight is only one planning constraint. Access, lifting direction, centre of gravity, reinforcement, cut lines and available equipment all influence how the slab should be divided. The lifting plan must be evaluated for the specific job.
