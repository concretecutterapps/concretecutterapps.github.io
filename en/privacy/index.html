Privacy policy

Concrete Cutter Calculator is developed by Concrete Cutter Apps with the aim of collecting as little personal data as possible. Core features can be used without an account or sign-in.

Locally stored information

Settings, language, units, calculation history and notes are stored locally on the device. They are used so the app can function and remember your choices.

Advertising

The free version may show ads provided by Google AdMob. Google and its partners may process technical data, advertising identifiers, approximate location and ad interaction data for delivery, measurement, fraud prevention and security.

Analytics and crash reporting

The app uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to understand general usage, detect errors and improve stability.

In-app purchases

Premium purchases are processed by Google Play. Concrete Cutter Apps does not receive payment card details.

Internet and third-party services

Core calculations run locally and do not require internet. Network access may be used for ads, purchase verification, analytics, crash reporting and other Google platform services.

Accounts and personal information

The app does not require an account for calculation features and does not knowingly collect names, addresses, phone numbers or email addresses through its core functions.

Children

The app is a professional tool and is not directed at children.

Data security

Local data is protected by the security mechanisms of the user’s device.

Changes

This policy may be updated when app features, data processing or legal requirements change.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to concretecutter.app@gmail.com.
