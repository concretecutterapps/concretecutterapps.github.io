Contact

For questions, bug reports, privacy matters or other feedback, contact us at:

When reporting an issue

Include app version, phone model, operating system, language, units, feature used and a brief description. For calculation issues, include entered values, expected result and actual result.

Screenshots

Screenshots are helpful when relevant. Make sure they do not contain personal information or anything else you do not want to share.

Privacy

Questions about data processing can be sent to the same address. See also the privacy policy.
