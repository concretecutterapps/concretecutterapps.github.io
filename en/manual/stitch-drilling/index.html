Stitch Drilling

Plan hole positions for stitch drilling along lines and around multiple shapes.

Inputs

Choose straight line, circle, rectangle, triangle, L-shape or free form/polygon. Enter geometry and drill diameter. Drill depth is optional. Closed shapes also allow start point and direction.

Results

The result shows hole count, centre positions and measurement/diagram guidance. When drill depth is entered the app can also provide relevant block-weight/material information and optimization suggestions where supported.

Field check

Always verify actual drill diameter, overlap, reinforcement, access and tolerances before drilling.

When stitch drilling is useful

Stitch drilling is used when an opening or contour is created from overlapping core holes, for example where saw access is limited or the shape does not suit normal straight cuts. Planning centre points in advance makes the final contour more predictable.

Overlap and geometry

The holes need enough overlap to avoid continuous bridges of material between drilling positions. Excessive overlap, however, means more holes and more work. The app shows centre positions and a diagram so the marking can be checked before the first hole is drilled.

Field check

Verify the actual core diameter, tolerances, reinforcement, drilling angle and access around the complete contour. For closed shapes, choose start point and direction so marking error does not accumulate unnecessarily around the shape.
