Weight

Calculate weight from shape, dimensions, material and construction layers.

How to use it

Choose a shape: core, concrete pipe, rectangle, triangle, trapezoid or free form. Select the material and enter the dimensions shown for that shape. Length units can be changed per field or together.

Inputs

Weight supports up to three material layers. Extra layers use material, thickness and density, which is useful for composite constructions.

Results

The result shows total weight and area. With multiple layers it also shows thickness, density and weight per layer. The calculation is saved to History.

Concrete Cutter

Rectangle calculations can be used without Premium access; the other shapes follow the app's current calculation-access rules.

When the weight calculation is useful

Use weight before a cut section is released, when planning slab division, and when handling or lifting methods must be assessed. The purpose is not only to produce a number, but to identify early when a section may be too heavy for the available method or site conditions.

Practical example

A concrete section measuring 1.0 × 0.5 × 0.2 metres has a volume of 0.10 m³. Using 2,400 kg/m³ as a planning density gives an estimated mass of about 240 kg. If the construction has several layers, the app can register the layers separately instead of relying on one average density.

Check before using the result

Density is a planning value. Reinforcement, moisture, toppings and material variation can change the real mass. Verify dimensions and construction layers, and do not treat calculated weight alone as proof that a lift or fixing arrangement is safe.
