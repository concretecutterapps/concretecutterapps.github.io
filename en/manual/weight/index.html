Weight

Calculate weight from shape, dimensions, material and construction layers.

How to use it

Choose a shape: core, concrete pipe, rectangle, triangle, trapezoid or free form. Select the material and enter the dimensions shown for that shape. Length units can be changed per field or together.

Inputs

Weight supports up to three material layers. Extra layers use material, thickness and density, which is useful for composite constructions.

Results

The result shows total weight and area. With multiple layers it also shows thickness, density and weight per layer. The calculation is saved to History.

Concrete Cutter

Rectangle calculations can be used without Premium access; the other shapes follow the app's current calculation-access rules.

Open the feature in the Concrete Cutter app to perform the calculation.
