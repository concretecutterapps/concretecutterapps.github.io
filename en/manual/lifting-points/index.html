Manual / Lifting points

Lifting points

Calculate centre of gravity and proposed lifting-point positions for a concrete piece.

Inputs

Choose rectangle, triangle, trapezoid or free form, then internal or external lift and an available number of lifting points. Enter the geometry in the preferred length unit.

Results

The result shows a calculated or estimated centre of gravity and coordinates for each proposed lifting point, with X from the left and Y from the bottom. It is saved to History.

Field check

This is geometric calculation support, not a lifting plan. Load, anchoring, reinforcement, lifting equipment, angles and site safety must be engineered and checked for the job.
