Manual / Settings

Settings

Choose language, measurement system, weight unit, material data and Premium/privacy options.

Use

App language can follow the system or use Norwegian, English, Swedish, Danish, German, Finnish, Polish or Dutch. Measurement system can be metric or imperial, with kg or lbs for weight.

Options

Built-in material densities can be edited and reset. You can also create custom materials with a name and density, then edit, rename or delete them.

Stored data

Settings also contains Premium purchase/restore, analytics/privacy choices where available, rate-app and About.

Set up the app before entering measurements

Settings control language, measurement system and weight unit across the app. This matters when several people share measurements because a clear unit standard reduces the risk of mixing millimetres, inches, kilograms or pounds.

Custom materials and density

Custom materials can be used when a job does not fit the built-in materials. Give them a clear name and density so the planning value is understandable later. Changing density affects new calculations; it does not rewrite historical results that have already been saved.

Privacy and Premium

Settings also contain available privacy choices, Premium purchase/restore and app information. These choices affect access and data use, but they do not change the basic geometric principles described in the manual.
