Overcut

Calculate required overcut and see corner-hole suggestions.

How to use it

Choose the saw-blade diameter. The app provides standard selections from Ø500 to Ø1600 mm. Then enter wall thickness in the preferred length unit.

Inputs

The result shows overcut for full feed and limited feed. Where the documented data allows it, the app also suggests corner holes using either the fewest holes or a smaller diameter.

Results

The result includes the selected blade and wall thickness and can be shared. The calculation is saved to History.

Concrete Cutter

Overcut uses the app's documented blade/depth data. If a combination has no table data, the app reports that it is not documented.

Why overcut should be planned

A circular saw blade has to pass the visible corner to cut completely through a square corner in a thick construction. Overcut should therefore be decided before cutting, especially where finished surfaces must remain visible or adjacent construction must not be touched.

Full and limited feed

The result separates full feed from limited feed because available cutting depth changes how far the blade must travel beyond the corner. If the required overcut is unacceptable, corner drilling or a different work sequence may be the better method.

Checks on site

Use the actual blade setup, wall thickness and available saw travel as the basis. The app calculation is based on documented blade/depth data; combinations without documented depth data are not presented as assumed safe values.
