Angle mount

Calculate the new drill-stand mounting distance for inclined drilling.

How it works

Enter the standard mounting distance at 90° and an angle from 1° to 89°. Concrete thickness is optional. Inclined drilling can pass the calculated angle and thickness directly to this feature.

Results

The result shows standard distance, drilling angle, new mounting distance and the difference from standard. With concrete thickness it also shows drilling length.

Practical check

The result is geometric. Check the stand, machine clearance, actual setup and measurements before drilling.

What Angle mount is for

Angle mount is used after the required drilling angle is known. It converts the normal 90° mounting distance into a new geometric mounting distance for the selected angle, making stand marking more consistent.

Connection with Angled drilling

When an angle is calculated in Angled drilling, relevant values can be transferred to Angle mount. This reduces re-entry of the same angle and helps keep the drilling calculation and stand setup tied to the same geometry.

Check before fixing the stand

The calculated mounting distance describes geometry, not physical clearance. Confirm the direction of tilt, the actual reference point on the stand, motor and core-bit clearance, the fixing method and that the measurement is taken from the correct side.
