Angle mount

Calculate the new drill-stand mounting distance for inclined drilling.

How it works

Enter the standard mounting distance at 90° and an angle from 1° to 89°. Concrete thickness is optional. Inclined drilling can pass the calculated angle and thickness directly to this feature.

Results

The result shows standard distance, drilling angle, new mounting distance and the difference from standard. With concrete thickness it also shows drilling length.

Practical check

The result is geometric. Check the stand, machine clearance, actual setup and measurements before drilling.
