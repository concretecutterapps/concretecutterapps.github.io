Slab division

Find a division that keeps pieces around a chosen maximum weight.

How to use it

Choose rectangle, circle, L-shape, triangle, trapezoid or free form. Enter the geometry, thickness and material.

Inputs

Enter maximum piece weight and safety margin. The default margin is 10%. For an L-shape, the notch must be smaller than the outer dimensions.

Results

The result shows piece count, total weight and the heaviest piece. Depending on shape, it also shows marking positions, cut-to-cut distances, piece weights and a drawing of the proposed division.

Concrete Cutter

The division is a calculation proposal. Lifting path, centre of gravity, reinforcement, anchoring and site conditions still require a separate assessment.

Open the feature in the Concrete Cutter app to perform the calculation.
