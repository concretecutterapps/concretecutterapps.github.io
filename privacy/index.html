Privacy Policy

Concrete Cutter is designed to collect as little personal data as possible. The app's core features can be used without an account or sign-in.

Data stored locally

Settings, selected units, calculation history and optional notes are stored locally on the device. This data is not sent to Concrete Cutter Apps and can be removed by clearing app data or uninstalling the app.

Advertising

The free version may display ads provided by Google AdMob. Google and its partners may process technical information, advertising identifiers, approximate location and information about interactions with ads to deliver, measure and protect the advertising service. Processing is governed by Google's own terms and privacy policies.

In-app purchases

Premium purchases are processed by Google Play. Concrete Cutter Apps does not receive payment card details. The app may verify purchase status through Google Play to activate purchased features and remove ads.

Internet and third-party services

Core calculations are performed locally and do not require an internet connection. Network access may be used for advertising, purchase verification and other platform services provided by Google.

Children

The app is intended for professional use and is not directed at children.

Data security

Local data is protected by the security mechanisms of the user's device. No solution can guarantee complete security, but the app is designed to limit data collection and transmission.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the app's features or applicable requirements change. A new version will be published on this page with an updated date.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent using the details on the contact page or the email address listed in the app store.
