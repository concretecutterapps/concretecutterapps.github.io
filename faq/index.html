Frequently asked questions

Can the app be used without internet?

Yes. Core calculations are performed locally. Internet access may be required for ads, purchases and Premium status verification.

Which units are supported?

The app supports both metric and imperial units. The selected unit is stored locally.

Which languages are supported?

Concrete Cutter supports Norwegian, English, Swedish, Danish, German, Polish, Dutch and Finnish.

Where is calculation history stored?

History is stored locally on the device. It is not sent to an account or a Concrete Cutter server.

Are results guaranteed?

No. The app is a practical calculation tool. Users must verify dimensions, material choices, assumptions and safety requirements before using a result in work.

What does Premium include?

Premium provides access to Premium features and removes ads in accordance with the offer shown in the app and Google Play.

Can a purchase be transferred to a new Android device?

A purchase can normally be restored when the new device uses the same Google Play account. Purchase status must be verified by Google Play.
