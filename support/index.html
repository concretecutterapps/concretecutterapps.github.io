Support

Find help with using Concrete Cutter, Premium purchases and common problems.

Calculations

Check that the correct unit, shape, material and all required dimensions are selected. Results are calculation aids and must always be assessed against actual site conditions.

Premium or purchase is not shown

Check that you are signed in to the same Google Play account used for the purchase and that the device has internet access. Then restart the app. Google Play may need some time to confirm purchase status.

Advertising

The free version may display ads. Premium removes ads once the purchase has been confirmed by Google Play.

History and settings

History and settings are stored locally on the device. They normally do not transfer automatically to a new device and may be lost if app data is cleared or the app is uninstalled.

Report a problem

Describe which screen you used, the dimensions and units entered, the expected result and what actually happened. Screenshots are helpful as long as they do not contain personal information.
